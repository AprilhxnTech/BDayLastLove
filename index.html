To My Dearest,

Happy birthday. I wanted to write something that goes beyond just "I love you" — because my feelings for you run much deeper than those three words can carry.

I know the world isn't always easy. Life demands a lot from us, and sometimes we face days that feel heavy. But I want you to know, with absolute certainty, that I am here. I am working hard, not just for my own dreams, but for a future where you are safe, happy, and at peace. I will always fight for us. I will always strive to be the man who protects your heart and supports your growth.

You are my home, and my greatest blessing. So today, on your special day, I want to leave you with a few reminders to carry with you:

A little reminder for you

  • You are deeply loved, exactly as you are.
  • Your feelings are valid, and you are heard.
  • You don't have to carry the weight of the world alone.
  • Your strength inspires me, but it's okay to rest.
  • You are my safe place, and I promise to be yours.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for staying, for trusting me, and for sharing this journey with me. May this year bring you endless peace, radiant health, and the fulfillment of every dream you hold close.

With all my love and devotion,
Your Man.
